# Feature → Benefit → Content Angle Matrix

Every row traces to a section in `FEATURES.md`. Use this as the primary fuel for generating
specific content — each row is close to a ready-made post/email topic on its own.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Public model record | Live, auditable W-L record + monthly calendar | Trust — no cherry-picked results | All | Landing page stat strip; monthly "here's our record" recap post |
| AI-written breakdowns | Claude narrative per pick (pitcher, bullpen, matchup) | Understand the *why*, not just the pick | Casual Fan, Sharp Bettor | "Read the reasoning, not just the number" |

## Seasonal content calendar hooks

- **Late summer (Aug):** Fantasy Draft Assistant + Cheat Sheet push — this is peak draft season.
- **NFL season (Sep–Jan):** Weekly regression-watch + start/sit content; weekly NFL picks.
- **MLB season (Mar–Oct):** Daily Steals + model-record recaps; email digest growth push.
- **Offseason lulls:** Lean on "how the model works" credibility content and public-record
  retrospectives.
