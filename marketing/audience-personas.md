# Audience Personas

Use these to tune tone, channel, and which feature to lead with. Most real users overlap more than
one persona — the app is built so a single subscription serves all three.

## 1. The Sharp Bettor ("Edge Eddie")

- Already bets MLB/NFL regularly, price-sensitive, skeptical of "picks services."
- Cares about: edge %, verdict logic, closing-line movement, public track record, sample-size
  caveats (e.g. "12+ IP for a starter" exclusion).
- Turned off by: hype, guarantees, anything that smells like a tout service.
- Lead with: the model methodology, the AND-gate exclusions, the public W-L record, the
  TRAP/PASS verdicts as proof the model isn't just always saying "bet."
- Best channels: X/Twitter, betting-focused Discords/subreddits, long-form breakdowns.

## 2. The Fantasy Manager ("Draft-Day Dana")

- Plays 1-2 fantasy leagues/year, most active in August–September (draft prep) and weekly during
  season (start/sit).
- Cares about: not getting blindsided in a live draft, VORP/tier clarity, injury/usage notes,
  who's trending, who's about to regress.
- Turned off by: generic ADP-only rankings with no reasoning, slow tools during a live draft.
- Lead with: the Draft Assistant's roster-need awareness, tier breaks, expected-vs-actual
  regression flags.
- Best channels: fantasy subreddits/Discords, seasonal timing (pre-draft push in Aug, weekly
  in-season pushes).

## 3. The Casual Multi-Sport Fan ("Weekend Warrior")

- Bets/plays casually, wants a simple daily habit, price-sensitive to a free tier.
- Cares about: quick daily digest, one clear "best bet," low friction (email > app-hunting).
- Turned off by: jargon-heavy breakdowns, paywalls with no free value.
- Lead with: the free daily pick, the email digest, the Steals feed as "here's today's best one."
- Best channels: email, the landing page, referral/access-code sharing.

## Cross-persona notes

- Every persona benefits from the AI chat assistant as an on-ramp — it lowers the bar to
  "understanding the board" for newer users while still giving sharp bettors a fast parlay-combo
  tool.
- The Portfolio/Tracker feature is a retention play across all three personas — it turns a
  one-off pick check into a recurring habit (checking your own record).
