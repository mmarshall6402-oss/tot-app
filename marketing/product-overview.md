# Product Overview & Positioning

## What T\|T actually is (as of this writing)

T\|T ("This or That") is a data-driven sports analytics platform covering **MLB and NFL
betting** plus a full **NFL fantasy football toolkit**, wrapped in a subscription product with
free and Pro tiers. It is not just a "picks" newsletter — it's a daily-use app with a live board,
a chat assistant, a personal bet tracker, and a draft-day tool.

Three product surfaces, one account:

1. **Betting picks** (MLB daily, NFL weekly) — statistical model + AI-written breakdowns +
   player props + a curated "Steals" feed of the day's best edges.
2. **NFL Fantasy** — cheat-sheet rankings (VORP, tiers, ADP value), a live Draft Assistant, and
   in-season start/sit signal via regression tracking and matchup adjustments.
3. **Portfolio / Tracker** — save your own picks, track your own P&L, chat with an AI assistant
   about today's board.

**One-liner:**

**Two-sentence:**

**Elevator (30 sec):**

> audit. On the fantasy side, the same modeling approach powers a weekly cheat sheet with
> real VORP and tier breaks (not arbitrary rank lists), and an on-the-clock Draft Assistant that
> tells you who to actually take given your roster needs and who's already gone. One subscription,
> one board, three sports use cases.

## What makes it different (use these as differentiator angles)

- **Shows its math, not just its picks.** The verdict system (CLEAN/BET/PASS/TRAP) and public
  model record mean the product can be honest about when it *doesn't* have an edge — a PASS is a
  feature, not a missing pick.
- **One model, two sports, one fantasy engine.** Most competitors are single-sport or
  picks-only with no fantasy tooling (or fantasy-only with no betting model).
- **Backtested, not vibes-based.** Both the betting model and the fantasy projections run against
  multi-season historical data (2022–2025 MLB game logs; historical fantasy seasons) before ever
  going live.
- **Draft Assistant reasons about *your* draft**, not just a static big board — it accounts for
  who's already been picked and what your roster still needs, live, pick by pick.
- **Transparent tiering** — fantasy tiers come from statistical gap-detection in the rankings,
  not a fixed "top 12 = tier 1" cutoff.
